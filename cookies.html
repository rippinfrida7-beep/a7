1. Principles of Minimal Cookie Utilization

FieldOuterPond operates under a strict principle of data minimization and digital transparency. We believe your online interactions with our outerwear atelier should be private, secure, and free from intrusive tracking networks. This Cookie Policy explains clearly how and why small text files (cookies) and browser local storage mechanisms are utilized on our domain to deliver high-performance functionality.

2. Essential Technical & Session Cookies

Essential cookies are strictly necessary for the core operational functioning of our website. These small files manage encrypted SSL/TLS session handshakes, remember items placed in your bespoke jacket inquiry cart, balance server network loads, and maintain secure user authentication states. Without these essential cookies, fundamental navigation, interactive sizing calculators, and checkout security cannot function properly.

3. Local Storage for Theme & Typography Preferences

To enhance your viewing experience, FieldOuterPond utilizes client-side HTML5 Local Storage to remember your chosen lighting theme (Highland Pine, Alpine Snow, or Midnight Storm) and in-article typography scaling preferences. This data remains stored exclusively within your local browser sandbox and is never transmitted to external marketing servers or advertising networks.

4. Anonymized Measurement & Performance Telemetry

We utilize Google Analytics 4 (Google Tag `G-0LY0HY7L01`) to aggregate privacy-compliant, anonymized telemetry metrics regarding website traffic, browser compatibility, page loading latencies, and device viewports. All visitor IP addresses are automatically pseudonymized at the point of capture, ensuring that no individual user profile or browsing history is ever linked to your real identity.

5. Browser Management & Opt-Out Controls

You retain complete autonomy to control, block, or delete cookies at any time via your browser settings menu (accessible in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge). Please note that disabling essential session cookies may impair interactive features such as the weather insulation configurator or digital concierge contact submission.

6. Governance Inquiries and Contact Information

If you have questions regarding our cookie practices or wish to review your digital privacy preferences, please reach our data team at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or contact us by phone at +1-888-777-5845.
